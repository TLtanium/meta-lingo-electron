### 刻意隐喻候选

**候选1：扩展隐喻——「发明是开门」（INVENTION IS OPENING DOORS）**
这是2024年信的核心框架，从S22贯穿到S40+：
- S22-25: **two-way door / one-way door** decisions — 定义框架
- S26: "the door is **unlocked**"
- S27: "trying to **open doors** that have **bolted** shut"
- S28: "the most important **keys** to **unlock** these **doors**"
- S40: "**unlock doors** that might have seemed **impenetrable**"
- S54: **DR**：读者需构建「门上锁→找钥匙→开门」的物理场景
- **→ PD（刻意）**，**高信心**：这是Jassy在2024年构建的最核心刻意隐喻

**候选2：「公司是船/航海」（COMPANY AS SHIP）**

**候选5：「寻找针在干草堆」（NEEDLE IN A HAYSTACK）**
